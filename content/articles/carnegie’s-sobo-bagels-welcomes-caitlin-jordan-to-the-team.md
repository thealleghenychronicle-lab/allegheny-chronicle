# Carnegie’s Sobo Bagels Welcomes Caitlin Jordan to the Team After Successful Interview

**CARNEGIE, PA —** Sobo Bagels & Deli has a new member of its team, and one Carnegie-area resident has officially received some very good news.

**Caitlin Jordan has been hired by Sobo Bagels following a successful interview Tuesday.**

The news comes after Jordan interviewed for a position at the popular Carnegie bagel shop, where she apparently made a strong enough impression to convince the restaurant that she belonged on the team.

According to sources familiar with the situation, the interview went extremely well.

Those sources also happen to include Jordan herself and the person writing this article.

Sobo Bagels & Deli, located at 221 2nd Avenue in Carnegie, is a locally owned restaurant serving bagels, breakfast sandwiches, deli sandwiches, coffee and other breakfast and lunch favorites. The restaurant describes itself as bringing an authentic New York-style bagel experience to Carnegie. More information about the restaurant can be found on [Sobo Bagels' official website](https://sobobagels.com/).

## A Successful Interview

Jordan's interview took place Tuesday, and by the end of it, the restaurant had apparently seen enough to make a decision.

**Caitlin got the job.**

While the exact details of the interview remain classified, sources say Jordan demonstrated several qualities that are generally considered useful in the restaurant business, including being friendly, dependable and willing to learn.

She also apparently answered enough questions correctly to avoid being immediately escorted from the building.

Instead, she was offered the position.

Jordan accepted.

## A New Chapter in Carnegie

Sobo Bagels has become part of Carnegie's growing local restaurant scene, offering customers everything from traditional bagels to breakfast sandwiches, deli items and coffee.

The restaurant's menu includes a wide variety of bagel options and breakfast combinations, along with sandwiches and lunch selections. Customers can check out the current offerings through [Sobo's official menu](https://sobobagels.com/lunch).

For Jordan, however, her next visit to Sobo will be a little different.

She won't be walking in as a customer.

She'll be walking in as an employee.

And that means there is now a very real possibility that she will become significantly more knowledgeable about bagels than the person writing this article.

## Sources Confirm Jordan Is Excited

People close to Jordan say she is excited about the opportunity and looking forward to starting her new job.

The position represents more than simply a new place to work. It's also a chance to meet new people, learn something new and become part of a local business that serves the Carnegie community.

According to [Sobo Bagels](https://sobobagels.com/), the restaurant focuses on fresh food, quality ingredients and creating a welcoming environment for its customers.

Jordan will now be part of creating that experience for the people who walk through the doors.

## The Bagel Perks

There is one potentially significant benefit that cannot be ignored.

**Caitlin Jordan now works at a bagel shop.**

This development could have serious consequences for her household.

Experts predict an increased likelihood of bagels appearing at home.

Cream cheese consumption could also rise dramatically.

Breakfast sandwiches may become a regular topic of conversation.

There is currently no evidence that Jordan's new employment will result in preferential treatment for her husband, but investigators are monitoring the situation closely.
