# Pittsburgh Startup Bank Sagehaven Secures Strip District Headquarters

**PITTSBURGH —** A proposed Pittsburgh bank is taking another step toward opening its doors, securing office space in the Strip District while it continues through the federal regulatory process.

Sagehaven Bancorp has finalized a 10-year lease for approximately 7,551 square feet of office space in Pittsburgh's Strip District, according to reporting published Wednesday by the *Pittsburgh Business Times*. The company is preparing for a potential launch in April 2027, pending regulatory approval.

[Source: Pittsburgh Business Times — August 26, 2026](https://www.bizjournals.com/pittsburgh/news/2026/08/26/sagehaven-hq-space.html)

The headquarters decision gives Sagehaven a physical base in one of Pittsburgh's most active business districts as the proposed bank works toward becoming a new financial institution in the region.

## A New Bank Taking Shape

Sagehaven was formed by a group of banking and legal professionals led by Brian Tabb, who is expected to serve as president and CEO.

The founding group also includes Angelo Innamorato, a former banking executive, and Jared Leland, a Pittsburgh attorney. The group submitted an application for a national bank charter in July.

Earlier reporting identified the proposed institution as Pittsburgh's first startup bank in roughly two decades.

[Source: Pittsburgh Business Times — July 14, 2026](https://www.bizjournals.com/pittsburgh/news/2026/07/14/sagehaven-startup-bank.html)

The proposed bank is targeting small and mid-sized businesses as well as mid- to high-net-worth customers. Its founders have said they see an opportunity to provide more specialized banking and financial services to customers they believe can be overlooked by larger institutions.

[Source: WPXI — July 17, 2026](https://www.wpxi.com/news/local/former-bny-huntington-execs-law-firm-partner-file-launch-startup-bank-pittsburgh/4HY55FEQHVBMRECQ6XPTUWQPPQ/)

## The Strip District Location

Choosing the Strip District places Sagehaven in a neighborhood that has become an increasingly important location for Pittsburgh businesses.

The area has attracted office, residential, retail and technology development over the past several years. Commercial real estate listings currently show a broad range of office properties available throughout the district, illustrating the neighborhood's growing office inventory.

[Source: PropertyShark — Strip District office market](https://www.propertyshark.com/cre/office/us/pa/east-pittsburgh/strip-district/)

The neighborhood's commercial growth has also changed the area's role within Pittsburgh's business landscape. The Strip District has increasingly competed with Downtown and other established office markets for companies looking for newer or differently configured workspace.

## Regulatory Approval Still Ahead

While the lease provides Sagehaven with a headquarters, it does not mean the bank has received approval to begin operating.

The company remains subject to the federal regulatory process associated with establishing a new bank. Its national charter application was filed in July.

That makes the planned April 2027 opening date a target rather than a guaranteed launch date.

For Sagehaven, the office commitment represents a concrete piece of the bank's development while the more consequential work of securing regulatory approval and preparing the institution for operations continues.

## What Comes Next

Sagehaven's progress will now be watched alongside the federal review of its proposed charter and the company's preparations for a potential 2027 opening.

If approved, the institution would enter a Pittsburgh banking market that includes major regional and national financial companies, while attempting to establish a more focused position among business and wealth-management customers.

For now, the Strip District headquarters is one of the clearest signs that the proposed bank is moving beyond the application stage and beginning to build the infrastructure needed for an eventual launch.

**The Allegheny Chronicle** will continue to follow Sagehaven's regulatory process and development as more information becomes available.
