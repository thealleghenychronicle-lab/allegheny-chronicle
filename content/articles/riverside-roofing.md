## A Neighborhood Built on Referrals

Riverside Roofing Co. didn't advertise for its first six years in business. Every job came from a
neighbor who'd seen the crew's work up close.

## What Changed

When a 2024 hailstorm tore through the East End, the company's response time became the story
homeowners kept repeating to each other.

### The Numbers

Response times, warranty terms, and crew size all shifted as the business grew — and its
customers noticed.

## Looking Ahead

The company is now expanding into commercial roofing across Allegheny County, betting that the
same reputation will follow it there.
