# NFL Ends Pro Bowl Games, Shifts Focus to Player Honor

**PITTSBURGH —** The NFL is ending one of its longest-running all-star traditions, eliminating the Pro Bowl Games while keeping Pro Bowl selection as an individual honor.

The league announced Wednesday that there will no longer be an AFC-NFC Pro Bowl game or a replacement event built around flag football and skills competitions. Instead, the NFL plans to place greater emphasis on the players selected as Pro Bowlers.

Beginning with the 2026 season, 88 players will be named to the Pro Bowl class in December. Unlike previous years, there will be no replacement selections for players who cannot participate.

[Source: NFL — August 26, 2026](https://www.nfl.com/news/nfl-elevates-pro-bowl-selection-game-s-will-cease-being-contested)

The selected players will still be recognized during an in-person celebration in Los Angeles around Super Bowl week.

[Source: Reuters — August 26, 2026](https://www.reuters.com/sports/nfl-ends-pro-bowl-selection-best-best-continue--flm-2026-08-26/)

## From Showcase Game to Player Honor

The decision represents another major change for an event that has spent years trying to find a format that players and fans would embrace.

The traditional Pro Bowl game was discontinued after the 2022 season. In 2023, the NFL replaced it with the Pro Bowl Games, which combined skills competitions with flag football rather than playing a traditional tackle-football game.

The league continued modifying the event in an effort to make it more appealing while reducing the physical risk associated with an exhibition game.

[Source: ESPN — August 26, 2026](https://www.espn.com/nfl/story/_/id/49735246/nfl-eliminating-pro-bowl-games-name-pro-bowlers)

The latest change takes the concept further by removing the competition itself.

Instead, the Pro Bowl designation will function primarily as a recognition of a player's performance during the season.

## The NFL Wants the Selection to Mean More

NFL executive vice president Peter O'Reilly said the league wants the Pro Bowl distinction to carry greater significance for players, their families, teams and fans.

That objective is reflected in the decision to limit the honor to the original 88 selections.

In previous seasons, players who were unable or unwilling to participate could be replaced, allowing additional players to receive Pro Bowl recognition. The NFL is eliminating that practice under the new system.

[Source: NFL — August 26, 2026](https://www.nfl.com/news/nfl-elevates-pro-bowl-selection-game-s-will-cease-being-contested)

The change could make the Pro Bowl designation more valuable as a career accomplishment because there will be a defined group of 88 players receiving the honor each season.

## A Business Decision as Much as a Football Decision

The Pro Bowl has faced a difficult problem for years: the NFL wants its best players involved, but those players have little competitive incentive to risk injury in an exhibition game.

That tension helped drive the move away from tackle football.

The league experimented with skills competitions, flag football and different scheduling arrangements, including moving the 2026 Pro Bowl Games into Super Bowl week.

The 2026 event was held in February at San Francisco's Moscone Center and featured an AFC-NFC flag-football matchup.

[Source: NFL — 2026 Pro Bowl Games schedule](https://www.nfl.com/pro-bowl-games/schedule)

Those changes ultimately did not lead the NFL to retain a game-based Pro Bowl format.

The new model removes the need to persuade players to participate in an exhibition contest while allowing the league to continue using the Pro Bowl brand as a way to recognize star players.

## What Happens to the Pro Bowl Brand?

The Pro Bowl has been part of the NFL's identity for decades, dating back to 1951.

Although the competitive game is disappearing, the league is not abandoning the Pro Bowl name.

Instead, the brand is being repositioned around the selection itself.

The NFL's voting process will continue to involve fans, players and coaches, meaning the annual selection will remain a way for the league's audience and participants to identify the players viewed as among the best at their positions.

[Source: Reuters — August 26, 2026](https://www.reuters.com/sports/nfl-ends-pro-bowl-selection-best-best-continue--flm-2026-08-26/)

That distinction could become particularly important for players whose contracts, reputations and career legacies are influenced by awards and postseason honors.

## Pittsburgh Players Will Still Have Something to Play For

For Steelers players, the change does not eliminate the possibility of earning Pro Bowl recognition.

Pittsburgh players will still be eligible for the annual selection, and the honor will continue to appear alongside other career accomplishments.

What changes is what happens afterward.

Instead of traveling to participate in an AFC-NFC game or a series of Pro Bowl Games competitions, selected players will be recognized through the NFL's new celebration format.

For a player, the Pro Bowl becomes less about showing up for an exhibition and more about what the selection itself says about his regular-season performance.

## The End of the Game, Not the End of the Honor

The NFL's decision effectively separates the Pro Bowl from the exhibition game that once defined it.

The league will continue selecting its top players, but it will no longer ask those players to compete against one another in an all-star contest.

That may be the clearest indication yet that the NFL believes the value of the Pro Bowl is in the recognition rather than the game itself.

The first 88-player class under the new format will be announced in December.

For the NFL, the challenge now shifts from finding a game format that players will embrace to making the Pro Bowl selection itself important enough to remain one of the league's premier individual honors.
