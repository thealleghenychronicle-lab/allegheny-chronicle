After putting up 28 points against the Green Bay Packers in their preseason opener, the Steelers were completely shut down Friday night, falling to the New York Jets **17-0**.

The loss drops Pittsburgh to 1-1 in the preseason and gives the team plenty to evaluate heading into its final exhibition game.

## Jets Strike First

New York wasted little time getting on the scoreboard.

Veteran quarterback **Geno Smith**, making his preseason debut after sitting out the Jets' opener with an ankle issue, led a 10-play, 69-yard opening drive that ended with a 13-yard touchdown pass to tight end Jelani Woods.

Smith completed **all seven of his passes for 65 yards and a touchdown** before leaving after the opening possession.

The Steelers' defense settled down after that opening drive, but Pittsburgh's offense couldn't capitalize.

## Will Howard Has a Rough Night

The game represented an important opportunity for rookie quarterback **Will Howard**, who made his first start.

Instead, Howard struggled.

He completed **10 of 15 passes for 74 yards** but threw two interceptions.

The first came in the red zone when Andre Cisco picked off Howard's pass intended for Roman Wilson at the Jets' 8-yard line, ending what had been Pittsburgh's best scoring opportunity of the night.

Howard was intercepted again later in the first half, with Samuel Womack III coming away with the pick.

The turnovers were particularly costly because Pittsburgh entered the red zone without being able to come away with points.

## Drew Allar Couldn't Get the Offense Going

Drew Allar took over after Howard, but the offense continued to struggle.

Allar finished **11-of-23 for 110 yards**, while Pittsburgh's offensive line had trouble protecting him.

The Steelers surrendered **four sacks** during the game, preventing the young quarterback from consistently finding a rhythm.

There was one encouraging moment when Allar connected with Lake McRee for a 38-yard completion, but explosive plays were few and far between.

Pittsburgh ultimately finished the night without a single point.

## Jets Defense Takes Control

New York's defense deserves plenty of credit for the shutout.

The Jets intercepted Howard twice, recorded four sacks and repeatedly forced Pittsburgh into difficult third-down situations.

Rookie edge rusher **David Bailey**, the No. 2 overall pick in the 2026 NFL Draft, recorded his first preseason sack against Howard.

Pittsburgh's offense simply couldn't find an answer.

The Jets added a field goal from Jason Sanders early in the fourth quarter to make it 10-0 before quarterback Brady Cook connected with Quincy Skinner Jr. for a 31-yard touchdown with 1:53 remaining.

That late touchdown put the final nail in the coffin.

## Not All Bad for Pittsburgh

Despite the ugly offensive performance, there were some positives.

The Steelers' defense kept the game within one score for most of the night after the Jets' opening touchdown drive. Several young defenders made plays, while Pittsburgh's special teams also helped with field position.

Punter **Cameron Johnston** averaged 50.5 yards on six punts, giving the Steelers' defense several opportunities to keep the game competitive.

But ultimately, the offense's inability to score overshadowed everything else.

## The Bigger Picture

It's important to remember that this is preseason.

The Steelers are evaluating players, testing combinations and determining who will make the final roster. One ugly August night isn't going to define the 2026 season.

Still, the performance raises legitimate questions.

Howard's two interceptions will get attention, particularly with the young quarterback competition being closely watched. The offensive line's struggles also need to be addressed after allowing four sacks.

At the same time, the Steelers showed in their 28-9 victory over Green Bay that this offense is capable of producing much more than it did Friday night.

The challenge now is finding consistency.

Pittsburgh will have one more preseason game against the **Buffalo Bills next Thursday** before turning its attention toward final roster decisions and the regular season.

For now, the scoreboard tells the story:

**Jets 17, Steelers 0.**

It wasn't the result Pittsburgh wanted in front of the home crowd, but the preseason is ultimately about discovering what needs to be fixed before the games start counting.

And after Friday night, the Steelers have plenty to fix.
