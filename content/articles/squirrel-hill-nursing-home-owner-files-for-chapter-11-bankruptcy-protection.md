# Squirrel Hill Nursing Home Owner Files for Chapter 11 Bankruptcy Protection

**PITTSBURGH —** The owner of a longtime Squirrel Hill nursing facility has filed for Chapter 11 bankruptcy protection as it works to restructure its finances and explore a potential sale of the property and operations.

Squirrel Hill PA Realty LLC filed for Chapter 11 protection on August 21 in the U.S. Bankruptcy Court for the Western District of Pennsylvania. The company owns the property associated with Squirrel Hill Wellness & Rehabilitation Center, a 178-bed skilled nursing facility at 2025 Wightman Street in Pittsburgh.

[Source: Pittsburgh Business Times/WPXI, August 26, 2026](https://www.wpxi.com/news/local/squirrel-hill-nursing-home-owner-seeks-chapter-11-bankruptcy-protection/2TOZWAZLJFAXDNQ7NZ35JR7BHM/)

The bankruptcy case is being handled as Case No. 26-22337. Court records show that a first-day hearing was scheduled for August 26 at the U.S. Steel Tower in downtown Pittsburgh.

[Source: Squirrel Hill PA Realty restructuring case records](https://cases.omniagentsolutions.com/documents/index?clientid=3801&tagid=1282)

## Owner Exploring a Sale

The Chapter 11 filing does not mean the nursing facility is immediately closing.

Michael Shiner, an attorney representing Squirrel Hill PA Realty, told the Pittsburgh Business Times that the company is focused on maintaining operations while beginning a process to sell the skilled nursing facility as a going concern.

In a sale as a going concern, a business is generally offered with the intention of continuing its operations rather than simply liquidating its assets.

[Source: Pittsburgh Business Times/WPXI, August 26, 2026](https://www.wpxi.com/news/local/squirrel-hill-nursing-home-owner-seeks-chapter-11-bankruptcy-protection/2TOZWAZLJFAXDNQ7NZ35JR7BHM/)

The bankruptcy case also includes SH Operator LLC. Court records show both entities filed Chapter 11 petitions on August 21 and sought joint administration of their cases.

[Source: Squirrel Hill PA Realty restructuring case records](https://cases.omniagentsolutions.com/documents/index?clientid=3801&tagid=1281)

## A Longtime Pittsburgh Facility

Squirrel Hill Wellness & Rehabilitation Center has operated in the neighborhood for decades and provides skilled nursing and rehabilitation services.

The facility's website lists its address as 2025 Wightman Street and describes services including short-term rehabilitation, long-term care, wound care and physical rehabilitation.

[Source: Squirrel Hill Wellness & Rehabilitation Center](https://squirrelhillwr.com/)

Pennsylvania state records list the facility as having 178 licensed beds and identify SH Operator LLC as its operator.

[Source: Pennsylvania Department of Health](https://apps.health.pa.gov/NCFFacilities/NCFOwnerLicensePull_202607.aspx)

The facility has also appeared on the federal government's Special Focus Facility candidate list. The March 2026 Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services list identified Squirrel Hill Wellness and Rehabilitation Center among facilities being monitored under that program.

[Source: Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services](https://www.cms.gov/files/document/sff-posting-candidate-list-march-2026.pdf)

## Bankruptcy Restructuring

Chapter 11 bankruptcy is designed to allow an eligible business to reorganize while continuing operations under the protection of the bankruptcy court.

The Squirrel Hill case includes several first-day motions concerning the continued operation of the facility. Court records show requests involving wages and benefits, cash management, insurance coverage, utility services and the protection of confidential resident and patient information.

[Source: Squirrel Hill PA Realty restructuring case records](https://cases.omniagentsolutions.com/documents/index?clientid=3801&tagid=1282)

The court scheduled the first-day hearing for August 26 in Pittsburgh, giving the bankruptcy process an early opportunity to address the company's requests while the case moves forward.

[Source: Squirrel Hill PA Realty case calendar](https://cases.omniagentsolutions.com/calendar?clientId=3801&sd=8%2F26%2F2026)

## What Happens Next

For residents and employees, the immediate focus will be on maintaining normal operations while the company works through the restructuring process.

The owner's stated intention to pursue a sale could eventually result in a new owner or operator taking control of the facility, although the bankruptcy filing itself does not establish that a sale will occur or identify a potential buyer.

For now, Squirrel Hill PA Realty's Chapter 11 case remains active in federal bankruptcy court, with the company seeking to reorganize while keeping the nursing facility operating.

**The Allegheny Chronicle** will continue to follow the bankruptcy case and any developments involving the future ownership or operation of Squirrel Hill Wellness & Rehabilitation Center.
